Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nyaya Stambha? ▼
Nyaya Stambha is an AI-powered legal tech platform that helps citizens find verified lawyers based on their legal issues. Our AI classifies your case and matches you with the right legal expert.
Is this platform free to use? ▼
The case classification and lawyer matching is free. Lawyer consultation fees are decided by individual lawyers. We also provide free legal aid referrals for eligible citizens under the NALSA scheme.
How does the AI classify my case? ▼
Our NLP (Natural Language Processing) engine analyzes your description and matches it against legal categories. If the AI is confident (≥60%), you get instant lawyer matches. Otherwise, a human expert reviews your case.
Are the lawyers on this platform verified? ▼
Yes. Every lawyer on Nyaya Stambha goes through a verification process including bar council registration check, identity verification, and background screening.
Can I use voice input in Bengali? ▼
Yes! Nyaya Stambha supports voice input in both English and Bengali. Select your language before clicking the microphone button. More languages will be added soon.
Is my data secure? ▼
Absolutely. All data is encrypted end-to-end using industry-standard encryption. We comply with data protection regulations and never share your personal information without consent.
What happens in an emergency? ▼
Use the red emergency button (always visible on the bottom-right corner) for immediate access to NALSA helpline (15100), Women Helpline (181), Police (100), and Cyber Crime (1930).
